Leadership & Roles

Our Story

We are new, and we do not pretend otherwise.

What we offer instead of a long history is a clear plan, proper papers and work you can go and see. We would rather show a planted field, a finished home or a delivered load than describe one.

FAANAK began in December 2025 with FAANAK Homes & Properties, followed by FAANAK Farms in January 2026 and FAANAK Energy Resources & Logistics in July 2026. Each is its own registered company; together they work the land in three ways.

When one of our companies makes a commitment, the whole group stands behind it.

Leadership & Roles

Who Does What

Each company is led by its own managing director, with group roles that keep all three on one standard.

  • Group

    Founder & Group Chairman

    Sets the direction for FAANAK Group and holds all three companies to one standard.

  • Energy & Logistics

    Managing Director

    Runs fuel supply, haulage and the fleet, and answers for every load and schedule.

  • Homes & Properties

    Managing Director

    Leads land, design and construction, and signs off title documents and handover dates.

  • Farms

    Managing Director

    Plans each season, from preparing the land to grading and supplying the harvest.

  • Group

    Finance & Compliance

    Keeps the accounts, registrations and filings of all three companies in order.

  • Group

    Partnerships & Enquiries

    The first point of contact for buyers, partners and suppliers through info@faanakgroup.com.
